FRIGHTWORKS: NIGHT OF 100 FRIGHTS - a PC port of Scooby-Doo! Night of 100 Frights by Loneranger419,
based on Scooby-Doo: Refrightened by JosiahG.

Version 1.0.3. Version numbers continue from Refrightened: its last release, 1.0.2, is where FrightWorks split off.

Needs your own GameCube disc image: USA v1.01 (GIHE78, revision 1), .iso/.gcm/.ciso. No game data is included.

1. Open "FrightWorks.exe".
2. Press Play. The first time, it asks for your disc image (Extras).

Settings: resolution, widescreen, 60 fps, anti-aliasing, HD textures, skip intro. Extras: disc, holidays, folders.
In game: F10 = settings, F11 = fullscreen / window, F2 = screenshot (saved in the Screenshots folder),
F1 = project page in your browser. Gamepads work right away.
Keyboard: WASD move, arrows camera, Space A, Left Shift B, R X, F Y, C Z, Q/E L/R, Enter Start, IJKL D-pad.

Saves, settings and mods are in data\UserData. To update, unzip the new release over this folder.
Coming from Scooby-Doo: Refrightened? Unzip FrightWorks next to the old folder. On first start the launcher offers
to copy your saves, settings, controls, mods and game files over; the old folder is left as it is.
